Open Cookie Database

This product includes data from the Open Cookie Database, maintained by jkwakman:
https://github.com/jkwakman/Open-Cookie-Database

The Open Cookie Database is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.
License text: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0

The bundled data has been transformed into a compact JSON index for offline use. Cookie descriptions remain in English; retention labels are translated where straightforward. No runtime network requests are made to obtain this data.
